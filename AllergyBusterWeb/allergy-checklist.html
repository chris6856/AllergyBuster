Food Allergy Safety Checklist
Everyday Situations

Use this checklist to stay prepared at home, school, work, restaurants, and while traveling.

🏠 Home & Daily Life
Read labels carefully every time
Avoid cross-contact in shared kitchens
Store safe foods separately
🏫 School / Work
Inform teachers, staff, or coworkers
Share a clear allergy action plan
Keep emergency medication accessible
🍽️ Restaurants
Ask about ingredients and preparation
Confirm cross-contact precautions
Avoid unclear or risky menu items
✈️ Travel
Pack safe snacks for the journey
Research allergy-friendly options ahead
Carry medication at all times
🚨 Emergency Readiness
Always have epinephrine available
Make sure others know how to respond
Wear medical identification if needed
