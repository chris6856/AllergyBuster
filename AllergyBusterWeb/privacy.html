Privacy Policy

How AllergyBuster collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Overview

AllergyBuster ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the AllergyBuster mobile application and website (allergybuster.com), how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

We designed AllergyBuster with privacy in mind. We do not require account creation, we do not sell your data, and we do not link your scans, searches, location or questions to you. Your allergy profile, preferences, saved products and shopping lists stay on your device.

Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following is stored on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Your allergy profile (the allergens you choose)
  • Your product preferences (for example additives, sugar and sodium limits, fragrance)
  • Products you save, your decisions about them (Approved, Needs review, Avoid) and the product information saved with them
  • Your shopping lists
  • Your free-scan count and app settings

This information is deleted when you uninstall the app.

How the App Looks Things Up

Barcode scans: when you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to our server (hosted on Cloudflare Workers), which looks it up in Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts and, for some foods, Edamam. To make repeat lookups faster, our server keeps the barcode and the public product information it found for up to 24 hours (6 hours if nothing was found). This cache is not linked to you or your device. If our server is unavailable, the app asks Open Food Facts and Edamam directly.

Label photos: photos of ingredient labels are read on your device using on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit). Photos are not uploaded to our servers and are not saved to your photo library. The camera's temporary image file stays in the app's temporary storage, which your phone clears automatically.

Product searches: search terms are sent directly to Open Food Facts, and to Edamam if Open Food Facts has no results.

Restaurant searches: your search text and, if you use "near me", your device's location are sent through our server to the Google Places API to find restaurants. Our server keeps the search results for up to 1 hour, stored with the location rounded to roughly 100 meters and without anything that identifies you. We don't otherwise store your location.

Ask Jeeves: the questions you type, along with recent messages in the conversation, are sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude API to generate replies. We do not store your conversations. Please avoid including personal or medical details you don't want shared with this service.

Daily tips: generated by Anthropic's Claude API. No information about you is sent.

Information You Provide Directly

Restaurant contributions: if you submit restaurant allergen data through our website contribution form, we collect the information you enter: restaurant name, location, menu item details, allergen selections, and any notes you include. This data is submitted voluntarily to help improve the community database.

Contact form: if you contact us, we collect the name, email address and message you provide, solely to respond to you.

Device and Usage Information

Like most apps and websites, our servers and hosting provider may receive basic technical information with each request, such as IP address, device type, operating system and app version. We use it only to operate the service, prevent abuse and fix problems. We may also use privacy-respecting analytics or crash reports that do not identify individual users and do not include the content of your scans, searches or questions.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account registration or collect login credentials
  • We do not upload photos you take within the app
  • We do not receive your allergy profile, preferences, saved products or shopping lists
  • We do not link your scans, searches, location or questions to you or build a profile of them
  • We do not collect payment information. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google

How We Use Information

Information we receive is used only to:

  • Operate and improve the AllergyBuster app and website
  • Look up products, restaurants and answers you request
  • Review and publish community-contributed restaurant allergen data
  • Diagnose technical issues, prevent abuse and fix bugs

Third-Party Services

AllergyBuster uses the following third-party services to function:

  • Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts: open product databases. Barcodes and search terms are sent to them, not linked to your identity.
  • Edamam: food database used when Open Food Facts lacks a product. Barcodes and search terms are sent, not linked to your identity.
  • Google Places API: restaurant search. Receives your search text and, if you use "near me", your location.
  • Anthropic Claude API: generates daily tips and Ask Jeeves replies. Receives the questions you type in Ask Jeeves.
  • Cloudflare Workers: hosts our server. Cloudflare may log standard request metadata under its privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: process in-app purchases under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Retention

Community-contributed restaurant allergen submissions are retained indefinitely to maintain the database. Barcode lookup results are cached for up to 24 hours and restaurant search results for up to 1 hour; neither is linked to you. We do not store Ask Jeeves conversations. Crash reports and analytics data, if collected, are retained for up to 90 days. Information stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

AllergyBuster is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including the right to access, correct, or delete information we hold about you. Since we collect minimal information and don't link lookups to you, most users have nothing to request. If you submitted restaurant allergen data and wish to have it removed, please contact us at privacy@allergybuster.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of AllergyBuster after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@allergybuster.com or use the contact form on our website.
